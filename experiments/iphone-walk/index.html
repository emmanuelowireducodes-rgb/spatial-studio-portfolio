What this prototype measures

The 8th Wall XR Engine SLAM pipeline provides a tracked camera position (x/y/z) and orientation quaternion (x/y/z/w), plus a tracking status. Position is relative to a session origin and may drift or become limited when the scene lacks visual detail. This is a technology evaluation, not a safety or navigation tool.

The camera stream is passed to the tracking engine. This page does not add the camera-feed renderer or a video background; the Three.js scene is configured as opaque. Confirm this behavior on a physical iPhone before relying on it.
